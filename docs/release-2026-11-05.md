# Release evaluation — 5 November 2026

**Assessment: not ready due to identified correctness and distribution blockers.** The frozen candidate improves pin recovery over the pre-improvement implementation on this new panel, but the silent-error non-inferiority gate remains inconclusive and a wrong MCU pad is promoted to high confidence. Redistribution clearance for bundled learned assets remains unresolved. No extractor changes were made in this phase; do not interpret these results as unattended production reliability.

Candidate: `5d2417c3bd93559255ccf40aee803411f841af24` (version 0.2.0). Protocol/freeze committed at `1fc810c`; panel/tools committed at `cd5012d`, before independent extraction. Pre-improvement baseline: `152c85a229be37fba9822fc2bbb6ee163b596749`. Recent baseline: `1934cda524831556ac42b570914274abe1e51660`. Later commits contain evaluation tools and documentation, not a revised extractor.

**63 designs, 40 source groups, 146 PDF pages, 16,341 scored truth pins.** All were new to the recorded corpus/cache history. The exclusion ledger contains 456 prior repository URLs; prior owners, forks, known ports and related designs were excluded. Grouping is conservative by owner, with exact-duplicate removal and 90% truth-net overlap clustering before extraction. All 81 acquired repositories were checked for GitHub fork ancestry; none was marked a fork. This does not rule out every unrecorded historical copy or shared circuit fragment.

The panel is a purposive sample of public designs. It is dominated by **62 generated KiCad 10.0.6 exports**, plus one verified native Altium PDF. ## Pairing and frozen procedure

The [registered protocol](release-2026-11-05-protocol.md), [freeze hashes](release-2026-11-05-freeze.json), [panel seal](release-2026-11-05-panel.json) and [complete input manifest](../bench/release-2026-11-05-panel.json) identify source revisions, source paths, PDF/truth hashes, groups, generators and verification decisions. The final panel SHA-256 is `fbdcefbdd40b9f7f25a8537b0abe8dfd3a2ceab69c1ccb6350c22d9924bb6511`.

PDF and netlist exports came from the same unchanged KiCad root source in the pinned, network-disabled Docker image. Source references were checked against exported components, including child sheets. Preliminary exports exposed older `Sheet file` spelling and silent empty legacy netlists; hierarchy detection and completeness checks were corrected **before any independent extraction**, and preliminary artifacts were retained separately. The final acquisition considered at most 20 lexical roots per repository, accepted at most two distinct circuits, and kept all verified distinct circuits. Empty or incomplete exports were quarantined rather than repaired.

The one native Altium case is UCSolarCarTeam/Driver-Controls-Hardware. All 71 connected carried-PDF net pin sets match the imported board exactly; all 402 carried pins exist in its pad registry. Connectivity comes from that PDF, while physical component references come from the board. Empty unit-only bookmarks U1A/U1B were represented by physical U1. Board Value fields are generic symbol identifiers, so values are unavailable for this case and are not scored.

**Serialization limitation discovered during post-score review:** the existing netlist exporter omits component-only pad metadata. Seven unconnected board pads are absent from the sealed Altium truth serialization (409 board pads versus 402 serialized known pads). Inspection of all six saved predictions proves that none uses those omitted pads: extra-pad membership is unchanged against the full board registry. The original board netlist, this audit and the frozen truth remain archived; no truth or panel was changed after scoring. This is a deviation from the intended full-pad-registry preservation and must be addressed in a future panel builder.

The acquisition records 185 KiCad root-export attempts and 14 native PDF attempts: 63 verified designs and 136 quarantined attempts. The original repository-level native ledger is retained; `native/design-decisions.json` lists the individual PDF decisions, including both EasyEDA variants. Native rejections include incomplete Eagle/EasyEDA reference/variant correspondence, two Altium board-import crashes, two PDF/board connectivity mismatches, unsupported Allegro import, and PDFs without sufficient native truth. Co-location was never treated as pairing proof. Full discovery trees, download blob/SHA-256 checks, source decisions and import/export logs are archived.

All implementations used Python 3.13.15 and identical locked dependencies, drawing-only extraction (`embedded=False`), default confidence cutoff 0.8, and no source-project library. Primary runs had no part library; secondary runs used the same two frozen KiCad/SparkFun indexes. No fold models or retraining were used. **`profile.use_learned(None)` restores bundled assets.** Installed model hashes and all 55 installed code/asset files match the frozen wheel/source; all three implementations have identical five learned binaries. Historical code came from Git archives; the candidate ran from its installed wheel.

The candidate scorer and KiCad reader score every saved implementation output. Nets of at least two distinct truth pins contribute to pin accuracy. Pair F1 excludes named power nets, while overall pin accuracy and exact nets include them. Unique pin-name aliases and the fixed symmetric-passive endpoint rules are applied consistently. Extras count numbered pins on known true references; unsupported references and their numbered pins are reported separately. Hidden and DNP pins remain in source truth. Value accuracy is conditional on common components with known values.

The scorer's “perfect design” means exact scored nets and no counted wrong nets;
it does not prove all references, values or unsupported extra nets are correct.
A design without scored silent errors may still have missing pins.

Each of 378 implementation/configuration/design attempts ran once in a fresh subprocess, with two workers, one native BLAS thread, rotated implementation order, a 4 GiB address-space ceiling and a 1,800-second outer timeout. All succeeded. Predictions, full issues, process logs and per-attempt CPU/wall/peak-RSS data are retained. Candidate and recent-baseline complete prediction files are **byte-identical in all 126 cases**, not merely equal on reported metrics. No panel prediction was repeated for determinism testing.

## High-confidence and physical-identity examination

All newly high-confidence false-pair designs versus initial were examined against their original PDF crops, pinned source symbol definitions and saved raw predictions. There are four designs, repeated in secondary mode, with no new extra pad identities or unsupported-reference pins. None is new versus recent; all complete outputs match that implementation.

| Design / page | Evidence and classification |
|---|---|
| Driver Controls / 5 | The PDF explicitly prints **BOOT0 94 → GND** and **NRST 14** below it. The predicted pin at the NRST drawing position is numbered **94**. Initial left it alone on a 0.5-confidence NRST net; candidate joins it to C5/R9 on the reset network at **0.85**, with no pin-addressed warning on U1.94. This propagates a wrong physical pad identity and creates new false pair connections. Passive-end alias choices cannot explain the MCU number. It is not evidence that the drawn reset and ground wires cross. |
| DSP ADAU1452 / 5 | Custom D306/D307 symbols use **1=A, 2=K**, opposite the inferred numbering. D306.2 belongs to 2V1 but is placed on VDD; D307.1 belongs to GNDD but is also placed on VDD. Raw net membership is unchanged; confidence rises **0.6 → 0.95**. Both retain explicit `pin_number_guessed` issues. This is existing pad reversal with confidence promotion, not a new geometric supply short. |
| Ghoul / 1 | Rotated NetTie_2 has upper +5V/Post pad **2**, but prediction calls it **NT3.1**. Raw membership is unchanged and an issue names both pads; confidence **0.6 → 0.95**. A net tie intentionally bridges copper networks, so the source-pad mismatch should not be described as a newly introduced unintended physical short. |
| Kobold / 1 | Rotated bidirectional TVS D17 has pad **2** on VBUS_IN and pad **1** at GND; prediction labels the upper endpoint 1. Raw membership is unchanged and the pin-guess issue remains; confidence **0.6 → 1.0**. The device is bidirectional, so this is a source pad-identity mismatch rather than proof of a new polarity fault. The frozen scorer does not grant TVS endpoint symmetry; its counts are retained. |

Full raw/aligned evidence, new false-pair ledgers, source records, cropped PDF images and the adjudication record are in local `review/` and `new-high-confidence.json`. The source-to-pin evidence distinguishes numbering/reference problems from geometry and Hungarian-alignment effects. Net confidence remains an evidence weight, not a probability; pin warnings must still be read even when a net reports 0.95 or 1.0.

Eight extra numbered identities remain: ABN6502 **R27.8/.9/.10**; DNS DriveBy **S1.1/.2**; BeeperBand **FB3.3, LD2.3, Y2.3**. They already exist in both baselines. There are also **27 unsupported component references carrying 184 raw numbered pins**, identical across versions, outside the true-reference extra-pin metric. Examples include power/value text interpreted as references (`+5V`, `V3S`, `LED0402`, `SHTC3`). These must not be hidden by quoting only the eight scored extras or 179 scored silent wrong pins.

The poorest candidate primary cases include STM32World `bat` (0/8 pins), AutoPilotPi `core` (11/80), and the Bad Thing of the Edge keyboard (97/148). None was removed. ABN6502 contributes 70 silent wrong pins and GB-BENCH-G1 another 57. Standalone hidden pins, repeated/custom bodies, values used as references, fragmented units and nonstandard pad identities remain correctness risks. The known development IRLML6402, PWR_LED/GND_PIN and Nolman limitations remain documented in the reliability report; this phase did not repair or retest them as fresh evidence.
